        Sync

        Keep the same texts and notes on your phone and computer. Readout keeps them in a secret gist on your GitHub account.

        1. Create a GitHub token. The form opens filled in: scroll down, tap Generate token, and copy the token.
        2. Paste it here and tap Connect. On your other devices, paste the sync key from this device instead.

        No Readout sync was found on this GitHub account. If sync is already on for another device, paste the sync key from that device instead.

        To add another device, paste the sync key into Readout there. Keep it private: anyone with it can read and change your synced texts.
